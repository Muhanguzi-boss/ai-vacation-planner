# Tokyo travel guide

Tokyo neighborhoods feel very different, so plan each day around one or two connected areas. Asakusa and Ueno work well together for temples, markets, museums, and traditional streets. Shibuya, Harajuku, and Shinjuku can be connected by train, but busy stations require extra navigation time.

Many ramen shops and convenience stores are budget-friendly. Carry a rechargeable transit card and follow quiet train etiquette. Small restaurants may have limited seating, so avoid peak meal times when possible.
