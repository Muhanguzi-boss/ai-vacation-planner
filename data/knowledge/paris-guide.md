# Paris travel guide

Paris is easiest to explore by grouping nearby neighborhoods. The Latin Quarter, Notre-Dame area, and Saint-Germain-des-Pres can be combined in one walking day. The Louvre and Tuileries Garden are close enough to plan together. Reserve popular museums in advance and use the Metro for longer cross-city journeys.

For a lower-cost trip, visit parks, neighborhood markets, and viewpoints with free entry. Local restaurants often offer a more affordable lunch menu than dinner. Keep valuables secure in crowded Metro stations and major tourist areas.
